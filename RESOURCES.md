## 5. Visa basics for international students

> **Not legal advice.** Rules and agency guidance changed during 2025–26 (see the CPT and H-1B notes below). Before you accept an internship, talk to your school's **DSO** (international student office) and, for H-1B questions, an immigration attorney.

### ⚠️ October 2026 updates

- **Green-card (PERM) suspension for 8 employers, including Adobe and Microsoft.** On Oct 8, 2026, the Department of Labor said it will not accept new or process pending PERM labor certifications (the first step of employer-sponsored green cards) for Microsoft, Adobe, Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL and Capgemini, citing ongoing investigations. No end date was given. This targets **green cards, not H-1B status**, and does not by itself stop internships or H-1B petitions. It is still a long-term signal worth weighing if you need sponsorship at those companies. Ask recruiters directly. ([TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/10/09/us-immigration-visa-fraud-green-card-h1b-j1-microsoft-adobe/), [Ellis](https://www.ellis.com/resources/perm-suspension-microsoft-adobe), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/10/vance-microsoft-h1b-layoffs-visa-data.html))
- **Proposed OPT fees: $70,000 initial, $30,000 for each later OPT period.** ICE published a *proposed* rule (Docket ICEB-2026-0100, Federal Register Oct 8, 2026) charging SEVP-certified schools per F-1 student who uses OPT. DHS notes schools could pass the cost to students or employers. It is **not in effect**: comments run 30 days from publication, and a final rule would come later and is expected to be litigated. The rule says it "is specific to OPT and does not address CPT", so summer internships done on CPT are not directly covered, but post-MBA OPT and STEM OPT would be. ([Federal Register 2026-20660](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-20660), [proposed rule text (PDF)](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-20660.pdf))
- **No blanket H-1B suspension.** As of Oct 9, 2026, no proclamation or USCIS/State notice suspends H-1B visas or existing status ([Orange Law](https://orangelaw.us/blogs/immigration-law/h-1b-suspension/)). The $100K H-1B payment remains in litigation (see below).

**CPT (Curricular Practical Training): the usual route for F-1 summer internships**
- Regulation [8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/8/214.2): CPT is authorized **by the DSO** and must be "an integral part of an established curriculum." Students with **one year or more of full-time CPT become ineligible for post-completion OPT.** The one-academic-year enrollment requirement has an exception for "graduate studies that require immediate participation" in CPT.
- **New in Aug 2026:** SEVP Broadcast Message 2608-02 (Aug 24, 2026) says CPT inside an *optional* elective does not meet the "integral" requirement, and that a curriculum requiring CPT must require practical training of **all** students in it, foreign and American. Ask your DSO how your school's internship course is structured. ([ICE BCM 2608-02 (PDF)](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/pdf/bcm_260802.pdf))
- Overview of CPT, OPT and STEM OPT: [Study in the States: training opportunities](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/training-opportunities-in-the-united-states).

**OPT (Optional Practical Training)**
- Up to **12 months** per education level. Your DSO recommends you in SEVIS, then you file Form I-765 with USCIS. For post-completion OPT you may apply up to **90 days before** and no later than **60 days after** you complete your degree. Any pre-completion OPT you use is deducted from the post-completion period. ([USCIS: OPT](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-opt-for-f-1-students))

**STEM OPT extension**
- **24 months** on top of post-completion OPT, if your degree's CIP code is on the DHS STEM list. The employer must use **E-Verify**, and you and your employer complete a **Form I-983** training plan. File up to 90 days before your OPT EAD expires and within 60 days of the DSO's recommendation. You may be unemployed for **up to 150 days in total** (90 on OPT plus 60 during the extension). ([USCIS: STEM OPT](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-extension-for-stem-students-stem-opt))
- MBAs usually qualify under CIP **52.1301 "Management Science"**, which is on the [DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List (PDF)](https://www.ice.gov/sites/default/files/documents/stem-list.pdf).

**H-1B basics** (all from USCIS)
- The annual cap is **65,000**, plus **20,000** for holders of a U.S. master's degree or higher. Cap-subject petitions have a start date of **Oct 1** or later. ([USCIS: H-1B](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations))
- Employers submit an electronic registration first. For FY 2027, registration ran **Mar 4–19, 2026** with a **$215** fee per registration. If a selection is needed, USCIS now runs a **weighted selection by wage level** (beneficiary-centric), not a pure lottery. USCIS reports that the FY 2027 regular cap has been reached. ([USCIS: registration process](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process))
- **$100,000 payment:** a Presidential Proclamation requires the payment for certain H-1B petitions filed on or after Sep 21, 2025. USCIS notes that a **Jun 8, 2026 court order vacated** its implementing guidance, and that DHS may still collect the payment if the order is lifted. This is in flux, so check the current USCIS page. ([USCIS: H-1B](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations))
