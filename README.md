## 📅 Recruiting timeline at a glance

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Pre-MBA summer** | Diversity conferences and career forums (Consortium OP, Forté, ROMBA), plus some pre-MBA company sessions |
| **Aug – Oct** | First Big Tech MBA postings go live: Amazon, Intuit, Microsoft finance and Apple posted Aug–Sep 2026. Many schools hold a recruiter quiet period early in the fall term |
| **Sep – Oct** | National conferences and career expos: NBMBAA, ROMBA, Prospanica |
| **Oct – Dec** | Company presentations, coffee chats, resume drops. More postings open, and some deadlines fall in this window (e.g. Google EMEA: Nov 13; Salesforce: Oct 20) |
| **Jan – Mar** | Peak interview season for Big Tech, then offers |
| **Jan – May** | Mid-size and late-stage tech post, and startups recruit last. Several ⏳ companies posted their 2026 MBA roles between Dec and Mar |

Details, sources and school-specific notes are in [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md#1-recruiting-timeline-school-agnostic).
